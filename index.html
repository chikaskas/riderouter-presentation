Overview

What is RideRouter?

RideRouter is a dedicated web and mobile platform helping cyclists and tourists navigate urban environments safely[cite: 19].

  • 🛡️ Safety Scores: Every available route is evaluated and scored[cite: 19].
  • 🗺️ Custom Routing: Pick between Safest, Scenic, or Fastest paths[cite: 19].
  • ⚠️ Crowdsourced Hazards: Riders can report road issues and potholes[cite: 19].
