Overview

What is RideRouter?

Ride Router is an app/web platform designed to help cyclists and tourists find the safest journey from one destination to another[cite: 3].

  • 🛡️ Gives every route a distinct safety score[cite: 3].
  • 🗺️ Pick between safest, scenic, or fastest routes[cite: 3].
  • ⚠️ Riders can report hazards like potholes[cite: 3].
  • 🪙 Earn coins for riding and feedback to exchange rewards[cite: 3].
