Overview

What is RideRouter?

RideRouter is a dedicated web and mobile platform helping cyclists and tourists navigate urban environments safely[cite: 3].

  • 🛡️ Safety Scores: Every available route is evaluated and scored.
  • 🗺️ Custom Routing: Pick between Safest, Scenic, or Fastest paths.
  • ⚠️ Crowdsourced Hazards: Riders can report road issues and potholes.

Environment

Air Quality & Exercise Trade-Offs

The Emission Factor

Traffic exhaust accounts for 26% to 60% of urban particulate pollution, concentrating toxic fumes in dense street grids[cite: 10].

The Health Verdict

Clinical models show cardiovascular gains heavily outweigh pollution risks—requiring over 1.5 hours of daily cycling in heavy smog before environmental exposure offsets fitness benefits[cite: 10].

Safety Data

Urban Corridor Risk Analysis

Speed & Severity Correlation

While bicycles represent a tiny fraction of total vehicle crash involvement, municipal transport data indicates fatality risks spike up to 85% on major arterial roads where average vehicle speeds exceed 80 km/h[cite: 11]. RideRouter actively routes users away from these high-speed arteries.
